Obsessionals

I have a theory on what I call "Obsessional" art. It's a simple belief that if you do something small, many many times, it becomes impressive. The audience can see the time and attention that went into the work, and is impressed by it. It's the first idea behind this passion project. The second, is about confused space. Various tricks are used to give a sense of 3D space to these 2D drawings. However these tricks deliberately conflict with each other, and the clearly flat sections of the drawings. The goal is to create an unsolvable problem that never resolves.
