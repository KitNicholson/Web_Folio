MONITOR G3

An experimental display font that is loosely inspired by various aesthetics and movements from around the turn of the century. It's not quite sure what it wants to be, as it tries to have the effortless minimalism of Gen-x Soft Club but also the exaggerated curves of early Apple computers. It's from the Appe Imac G3, released in 1998, that this font draws it's name.
