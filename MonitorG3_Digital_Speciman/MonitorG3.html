MONITOR-G3

Monitor-G3 comes from a combination of influences from around the turn of the century. A mix of early Apple computers (specifically the iMac G3) and an aesthetic known as Gen X Soft Club. It's looking back to a time when computers were still new, and big, and round, and exciting.
